Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide the Service, Spendly collects only the information it needs:

  • Account information — your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic technical data — such as browser type and access times, used to keep the Service secure and working.

We do not ask for or store bank account numbers, card details or other payment credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store, display and summarise your expenses.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the Service.
  • Contact you about important changes to your account or these policies.

We never sell your personal or financial data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our database and protected with reasonable technical and organisational safeguards. Passwords are stored only in hashed form. While we work to protect your information, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on a small number of trusted providers, such as hosting and font delivery services, to operate the Service. These providers may process limited technical data (for example, your IP address) on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with third parties unless required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to access, correct or delete your data, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
